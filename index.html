She said it. Here's the other side.

Every quote below comes from Hamilton's own campaign website. Every response is ours.

Rural character

“fiercest advocate” for District 62's history, farmland and rural character— hamiltonforvirginia.com/priorities

The fiercest advocate filed zero bills on data centers, transmission corridors, conservation easements or farmland protection in her first session, while Virginia debated more than 60 data center bills. She did agree to sign Orange County's letter against Valley Link. A signature on a letter isn't legislation.

Government intrusion

Will vote to reduce government intrusion and oppose “unconstitutional mandates”— hamiltonforvirginia.com/priorities

Her HB 537 would have ordered the state Board of Health to ban fluoride in every public water system in Virginia, overriding every local water authority that chose it. The Virginia Dental Association opposed it. A House subcommittee tabled it 9–0, so not even her own party backed it. That's a statewide mandate, not freedom.

Economic freedom

Lower taxes, less regulation, families keeping more of what they earn— hamiltonforvirginia.com/priorities

Not one of her bills cut anyone's taxes or their electric bill, even though rising power costs top Virginia voters' worries, and most of them blame data centers. One of her bills, HB 536, would have authorized impact fees on new residential development, which is a new charge on the people building homes here.

Reproductive rights

Opposed “Enshrining abortion in our Virginia Constitution”— hamiltonforvirginia.com/priorities

On November 3, 2026, Virginia voters approved the Right to Reproductive Freedom Amendment by a wide margin. Hamilton spent her session on two abortion bills that went nowhere. Personal medical decisions belong to patients and their doctors, not to a delegate in Richmond.

Public schools

School choice so that “government schools” must compete for students— hamiltonforvirginia.com/priorities

In rural counties, the public school is often the only school for miles, and one of the biggest employers. Voucher-style “choice” sends tax dollars out of those buildings. If our recovery rankings are bad, the answer is to invest in the schools our kids actually attend, not to drain them.

Farming

Criticizes “ludicrous climate initiatives” in agriculture— hamiltonforvirginia.com/priorities

Farmers here deal with drought years, flash floods and heat in the fields. Conservation and resilience programs are tools that keep family farms in business. Calling them ludicrous isn't standing up for farmers. It means walking away from the help they use.

Guns

Will oppose new gun legislation and seek repeal of existing laws— hamiltonforvirginia.com/priorities

Most gun owners in this district are responsible hunters and homeowners who also support sensible limits. Hamilton's HB 540 tried to carve an exception into Virginia's restricted-location carry rules, and it was killed. Repealing laws across the board is not what most gun owners here are asking for.

Priorities

“Virginia is for Patriots”— hamiltonforvirginia.com

Virginia is for everyone who lives here. Her campaign site's own page description still advertises “Cameron Hamilton” for U.S. Congress in VA-7. When the campaign site can't keep straight who is running for what, it's fair to ask whose priorities this seat really serves.
