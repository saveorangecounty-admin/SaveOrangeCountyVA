The Issues

What's on the table

Transmission

Joshua Falls–Yeat, 765kV

Valley Link — a joint venture of Dominion Energy, Transource and FirstEnergy — proposes about 115 miles of 765kV line from Campbell County to Culpeper County, crossing up to nine counties including Orange. Structures run roughly 135–160 feet on a 200-foot right-of-way. Approval rests with the State Corporation Commission, not the county.

Zoning

The Technology District

Adopted December 2025. It is a floating district — it designates no specific sites, sets no cap on total acreage, and places no limit on how many parcels countywide may be rezoned for data centers. In January 2026 the Board initiated ZTA 26-01 to remove data centers as a by-right use, routing future proposals through special use permits.
